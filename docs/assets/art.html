Three verdicts, one rule

A verdict is issued only on consistent evidence. Otherwise Tally investigates, and if it still can't prove anything, it asks.
PASS

Every ordered item ended in the box and nothing else did. All five checks hold.

FAIL

The evidence is consistent but the box is wrong: “missing: 0 lays in box, order says 1”.

NEEDS REVIEW

Tracks are inconsistent (ID switch, teleport, ambiguous product). Glimmer repairs under the gate, or a human signs off.
